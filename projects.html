ECE 212 — LED State Machine Circuit

For our final project, we were tasked with designing and building two independent state machines controlling sequences of LEDs. These state machines would be powered by a 555 timer, with an individual input to switch between the two. I decided to make a 3-state 'X' pattern and a 4-state 'O' pattern.
